Overview

Job-ready skills, not just certificates

TecheVision offers IT training and job placement assistance to help people launch and grow careers in technology.

We focus on the same skills we use on client projects — data engineering, Java and Python development, cloud, data analytics, and QA — so what you learn maps directly to what employers actually need. Alongside training, we provide placement assistance: resume and interview preparation, guidance, and help connecting with real opportunities.

Whether you're a recent graduate, changing careers, or upskilling in your current role, our goal is practical, job-ready skills you can put to work right away. Based in Ashburn, Virginia, we work with learners across Northern Virginia and remotely nationwide.

FAQ

Common questions

Do you guarantee a job?

We provide job placement assistance — including resume and interview preparation, guidance, and help connecting with opportunities — but we don't promise a specific outcome. Your effort and market conditions matter.

What skills do you train for?

The in-demand areas we work in every day: data engineering, Java and Python development, cloud, data analytics, and QA/test automation.

Is the training remote or in person?

We work with learners remotely and across Northern Virginia. Reach out and we'll talk through what fits your goals and schedule.

Do you help with interview prep and resumes?

Yes — resume and interview preparation are part of the placement assistance we offer, so you can present your skills with confidence.
