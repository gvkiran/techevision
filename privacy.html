Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TecheVision ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit techevision.com or contact us through the site.

Information we collect

Information you provide. When you use our contact form or apply for a job, we collect the details you submit — such as your name, email address, phone number, the role you're applying for, work-authorization details, and any message or information you include.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, we collect limited technical and usage data — such as your approximate location, device and browser type, pages viewed, and how you interact with the site — through cookies and analytics (see below).

How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiries and provide the services you request.
  • To review and process job applications.
  • To understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it.
  • To maintain the security and proper functioning of the site.

Cookies & analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand site traffic and usage. Analytics cookies are only loaded after you accept them via our cookie banner; if you decline, these cookies are not set. We also enable IP anonymization. You can change your choice at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

How your information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us operate the site and communicate with you, including:

  • FormSubmit — processes and delivers our form submissions by email.
  • Google Analytics — provides anonymized usage statistics.
  • Vercel — hosts the website.

These providers process data on our behalf and under their own privacy policies. We may also disclose information where required by law.

Data retention

We keep the information you submit for as long as needed to respond to you, consider your application, or as required for legitimate business or legal purposes, after which it is deleted or anonymized.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information you've provided to us. To make a request, email contact@techevision.com and we'll respond within a reasonable timeframe.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact us

Questions about this policy? Reach us at contact@techevision.com or +1 (559) 966-1123, Ashburn, Virginia, USA.

This policy is provided for general information and is not legal advice. For a policy tailored to your specific obligations, consult a qualified professional.
