Overview

From raw data to real decisions

Dashboards and insight that turn your data into better decisions.

Data only helps when people can act on it. We define the metrics that matter, build clear dashboards and reports, and help your teams self-serve the answers they need — so decisions are based on evidence, not guesswork. From executive KPIs to ad-hoc analysis, we make your data usable.

We work with Power BI and Tableau, backed by SQL and Python for data preparation and analysis, and we focus as much on clarity and storytelling as on the numbers. Based in Ashburn, Virginia, we serve clients across Northern Virginia and remotely nationwide.

Most organizations don’t lack data — they lack a single, trusted view of it. Sales numbers live in one system, finance in another, and the weekly report is a spreadsheet someone rebuilds by hand. Different teams quote different figures for the same metric, and meetings turn into debates about whose number is right. Our analytics work starts by agreeing on clear definitions for the measures that matter, then connecting the sources behind them so everyone reads from the same page.

Good analytics is also about people. A dashboard nobody opens is just cost. We design reporting around the questions each audience actually asks — what an executive needs at a glance, what a manager needs to act on this week, what an analyst needs to dig deeper — and we document how every metric is calculated so the numbers can be explained, checked and trusted.

When it helps

Signs it’s time to invest in analytics

Reports built by hand

Someone spends hours every week copying data between spreadsheets to produce the same report. We automate that refresh so the time goes into analysis instead of assembly.

Numbers that don’t match

Sales, finance and operations each report a different figure for revenue or customers. We agree one definition per metric and build reporting on top of it.

Dashboards nobody uses

You already pay for Power BI or Tableau, but the dashboards are cluttered or out of date. We redesign them around the decisions people actually make.

No clear KPIs

Goals are set, but nobody can say week to week whether you’re on track. We help define a small set of KPIs and the reporting to track them.

Questions take days to answer

Every new question becomes a request to one busy analyst. Well-modeled, self-serve reporting lets teams answer routine questions themselves.

Planning by gut feel

Budgets and staffing are based on last year plus a guess. Trend analysis and forecasting give you an evidence-based starting point.

Analytics & engineering

How analytics fits with your data platform

Data analytics and data engineering are two halves of the same job. Engineering moves, cleans and stores data reliably; analytics turns that data into metrics, dashboards and answers. If your data is already in good shape — in a warehouse, a database or well-maintained files — we can start on reporting right away. If it’s scattered or unreliable, we’ll tell you, and our data engineering team can build the foundation first so your dashboards rest on numbers you can trust.

Because both teams work side by side, you don’t have to coordinate separate vendors or explain your business twice. And if you already have an internal data team, we can work alongside them — adding analytics capacity, reviewing existing reports, or helping set up standards for how metrics are defined and documented.

FAQ

Common questions

What does a data analytics engagement look like?

We define the metrics that matter, build dashboards and reports, and help your team self-serve insight — turning raw data into decisions.

Which BI tools do you work with?

Power BI and Tableau most often, backed by SQL and Python for data preparation and analysis.

Can you help us define the right KPIs?

Yes — we work with your teams to define meaningful KPIs and metrics, then build the reporting to track them.

Do we need a data warehouse before we start?

Not necessarily. If your data is in reasonable shape, we can report directly from your existing databases and files. If it is scattered or unreliable, we will recommend building a proper foundation first, which our data engineering team can help with.

Can you improve the dashboards we already have?

Yes. We can review existing Power BI or Tableau reports, fix inaccurate or slow ones, simplify cluttered layouts, and document how each metric is calculated.

Will our team be able to maintain the reports after you hand them off?

That is the goal. We document data sources and metric definitions, and walk your team through how the reports work so they can update and extend them with confidence.

Do you work with clients outside Virginia?

Yes. We are based in Ashburn, Virginia and work with clients across Northern Virginia and remotely nationwide.
