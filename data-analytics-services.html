Overview

From raw data to real decisions

Dashboards and insight that turn your data into better decisions.

Data only helps when people can act on it. We define the metrics that matter, build clear dashboards and reports, and help your teams self-serve the answers they need — so decisions are based on evidence, not guesswork. From executive KPIs to ad-hoc analysis, we make your data usable.

We work with Power BI and Tableau, backed by SQL and Python for data preparation and analysis, and we focus as much on clarity and storytelling as on the numbers. Based in Ashburn, Virginia, we serve clients across Northern Virginia and remotely nationwide.
